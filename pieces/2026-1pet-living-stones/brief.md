# Brief

## Reader
A believer who has paid a social price for following Christ: left out, mocked, quietly passed over. No Greek, no seminary. Reads on a phone.

## Big idea (one sentence)
People may reject you as they rejected Jesus, but God has chosen and honored him, and everyone who comes to him shares his honor and his calling.

## What the reader should believe and do afterward
- Believe: my worth is settled by God's verdict on Christ, not by my neighbors' verdict on me.
- Do: treat Sunday worship as priestly service; speak of what God has done; stop seeking honor from those who withhold it.

## Grounding in the research (dossier, passages/1pet/2.4-10.md)
- Meaning statement (E10) and thesis (Stage 5): honor in union with Christ the rejected-and-vindicated stone; corporate priesthood; Israel's titles applied by analogy and mercy.
- Syntax decisions used: οἰκοδομεῖσθε indicative, divine passive (God builds); house = temple (4:17); "the honor is for you who believe" (2:7); stumbling appointed, disobedience its cause (2:8, held as more natural, not proven).
- Q8: a foundation-level cornerstone.
- H17 lasting principles 1–4 and specific applications (derived authority).
- H18 limits: do not say the church is Israel or "new Israel"; do not say Israel lost its promises; Rom 11:18 caution.

## Things not to say
- "Peter, the rock, writes about stones" (the text never makes that link).
- "Capstone" or "keystone."
- Reprobation stated as proven doctrine from 2:8.
- Any personal story in Jack's voice that he has not told.
