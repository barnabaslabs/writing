# Outline

Hook: the pain of being left out on purpose; Peter's readers (4:4). Question: what holds them steady? Peter answers with a description, not advice.

1. **The stone people threw away (2:4, 6–7).** Claim: Jesus was rejected by people and chosen by God. Evidence: Ps 118:22; Jesus' own use (Mark 12:10); Peter's sermon (Acts 4:11). Explain: define cornerstone (foundation stone the building rests on). Transition: what God did with the stone, he does with those who come to it.
2. **Living stones in God's house (2:4–5).** Claim: God is building believers into his temple and priesthood. Evidence: divine passive; house = God's house (4:17); priesthood, spiritual sacrifices through Jesus Christ. Explain: example of a stone that only means something in a wall; contrast with "my private faith." Transition: but the same stone does two opposite things.
3. **Honor or stumbling (2:6–8).** Claim: the stone divides people by their response. Evidence: Isa 28:16 "not put to shame"; "the honor is for you who believe"; Isa 8:14 stumbling, because they disobey the word. Explain: shame answered by honor. Transition: Peter turns from them to "you."
4. **What God calls you (2:9–10).** Claim: by mercy God gives Gentile believers the titles and calling he gave Israel. Evidence: Exod 19:5–6; Isa 43:20–21; Hosea's "not my people." Explain: define "royal priesthood"; guardrail on Israel (Rom 11:18). Purpose: proclaim his excellencies.

Application (three, tied to phrases): God's verdict over the crowd's ("chosen and precious"); gathering as priestly work ("spiritual sacrifices"); telling what he has done ("proclaim the excellencies").

Close: return to the hook. You may be the stone others pass over. So was he.
